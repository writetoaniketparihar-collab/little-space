# Support

## Bugs and feature requests

Use the [issue templates](https://github.com/writetoaniketparihar-collab/little-space/issues/new/choose). Include the app version, macOS version, what you expected and what happened. Reproduce the issue with fictional note text where possible.

Everything in an issue is public, including screenshots and attachments. Remove names, email addresses, private links and note contents. Do not attach your vault, Keychain exports, payment details, receipts or unreviewed diagnostic logs.

## Purchases, refunds and private help

Email [hello@littlespace.pro](mailto:hello@littlespace.pro). Do not post purchase details in GitHub Issues. The [customer portal](https://polar.sh/little-space/portal) provides downloads for existing customers. Purchase and refund terms are on [littlespace.pro/terms](https://littlespace.pro/terms).

## Security issues

Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) to report privately. Do not open a public bug report describing an unpatched vulnerability.

## Troubleshooting safely

Back up important notes before changing sync settings. Keep your vault, app data and Keychain entries. Support instructions should never require you to publish private notes, reveal passwords, or disable macOS security protections.
