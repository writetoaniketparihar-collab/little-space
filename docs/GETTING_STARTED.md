# Get started

## Install

1. Buy through [littlespace.pro](https://littlespace.pro/) or open the [customer portal](https://polar.sh/little-space/portal) if you already own Little Space.
2. Download the installer, open the disk image and drag Little Space into Applications.
3. Open the app from Applications. Hover a coloured tab at the screen edge to preview a note, then click inside to write.

Keep macOS security protections enabled. If macOS rejects the installer, contact support rather than disabling Gatekeeper or running commands from an issue comment.

## Everyday use

- Click a note's name to rename it, or its coloured dot to change the colour.
- Use Add task to make a checkbox, and Reminder to choose a time. A due reminder makes the note or its tab glow.
- Use the formatting controls for bold, italics, bullets and alignment.
- Use the microphone for speech recognition. It requires microphone and speech permissions and a supported on-device recognition language.
- Open the note's gear menu or the menu bar for the in-app guide. Appearance and Open at login are in Settings.

## Optional iPhone sync

Enable Notes and Reminders in iCloud on your Mac and iPhone with the same Apple Account. Turn on iPhone sync in Little Space and grant access when macOS asks.

Writing is copied to the Little Space folder in Apple Notes. Tasks use Little Space lists in Apple Reminders. Notes saved only under On My Mac will not appear on your iPhone. iCloud availability and Apple permissions affect sync.

Use the in-app guide for the current sync instructions and supported editing behaviour. Back up important notes before troubleshooting sync. Do not delete your vault, app data or Keychain entries to reset the app.

## Update

Quit Little Space, download the update through the customer portal and replace the app in Applications. Keep the existing app data and Keychain entries. Optional update notifications do not install updates automatically.
