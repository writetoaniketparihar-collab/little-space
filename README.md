# Little Space

Sticky notes on the edge of your Mac. Hover a tab to open a note, capture a thought, then return to your work.

[Website and interactive demo](https://littlespace.pro/) · [Get started](docs/GETTING_STARTED.md) · [Report a bug](https://github.com/writetoaniketparihar-collab/little-space/issues/new/choose)

## What it does

- Keeps notes within reach as coloured tabs on the edge of the screen.
- Supports checklists, reminders, links and text formatting.
- Turns speech into text using supported on-device speech recognition.
- Optionally syncs writing to Apple Notes and tasks to Apple Reminders, so they can reach your iPhone through iCloud.

## Requirements and purchase

Little Space requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 14 or later. There is no separate Little Space iPhone app. Optional iPhone sync requires Notes and Reminders enabled in iCloud on both devices, using the same Apple Account, plus the requested macOS permissions.

Little Space is a paid app, sold directly through [littlespace.pro](https://littlespace.pro/). Current pricing and purchase terms are shown there. Existing customers can get their download through the [Polar customer portal](https://polar.sh/little-space/portal), using the email they bought with.

Installers are signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple. See [downloads and installer verification](docs/DOWNLOADS.md). Notarization is not a guarantee that software has no vulnerabilities.

## Privacy and support

The local notes vault is encrypted, with its key stored in macOS Keychain. The app does not require an account. Optional Apple sync creates additional copies in Apple Notes and Reminders. Optional update checks contact the Little Space website. Read [privacy and permissions](docs/PRIVACY.md) for the details and limits.

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/writetoaniketparihar-collab/little-space/issues) for bugs and feature requests. Issues are public: use sample text, and remove personal information from screenshots. For purchase questions or private support, email [hello@littlespace.pro](mailto:hello@littlespace.pro). Report vulnerabilities privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
